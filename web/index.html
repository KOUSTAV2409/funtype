Modes & Mechanics

Engineered for real workflow breaks

Designed for keyboard athletes and computer workers who want tactile catharsis without cognitive friction.

Arcade Catharsis

Stress Shredder

Real engineering stressors like merge conflict, prod incident, and segfault descend gracefully. Typing them vaporizes them into clean particle bursts, lowering your workplace stress gauge to zero.

Key: 1 or F1 Timer: 15s / 30s / 60s / Free Fall
Physiological Reset

Zen Flow & Breathing Orb

Synchronizes typing cadence with a 12-second visual respiration guide (Box Breathing: 4s Inhale, 2s Hold, 4s Exhale, 2s Rest) paired with philosophical reflections to down-regulate heart rate and mental strain.

Key: 2 or F2 Zero error penalties
Targeted Muscle Memory

Weak Keys Targeter

Instead of mindlessly typing random vocabulary, FunType isolates your most frequent mistypes and instantly synthesizes custom drills around your weak finger transitions via Ctrl + M.

Key: Ctrl + M Live WPM sparklines

Configuration

Instant Desktop Integration

The installer automatically configures your window rules, or paste them manually below.

# ~/.config/hypr/hyprland.conf bind = SUPER, Y, exec, ~/.local/bin/funtype windowrulev2 = float, class:^(funtype)$ windowrulev2 = size 1040 660, class:^(funtype)$ windowrulev2 = center, class:^(funtype)$
