Operating Modes

Three specialized engines for mental recovery

Whether you want aggressive catharsis, steady breathing cadence, or targeted speed drills.

01 Arcade

Stress Shredder

Real engineering blockers descend gracefully (merge conflict, prod incident, segfault). Typing them vaporizes each stressor into clean spark particle explosions and resets your workplace stress gauge to zero.

Integration

Automated window manager configuration

The installer automatically configures your floating window rules, or paste them manually below.

# ~/.config/hypr/hyprland.conf (or ~/.config/hypr/bindings.lua in Omarchy) bind = SUPER, Y, exec, ~/.local/bin/funtype windowrulev2 = float, class:^(funtype)$ windowrulev2 = size 1040 660, class:^(funtype)$ windowrulev2 = center, class:^(funtype)$
